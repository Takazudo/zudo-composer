## Who we are

Sample Studio is four people: a strategist, a designer, a writer and a developer. We have worked together for six years, mostly with small teams in education, public services and independent retail.

Each of us leads one part of a project, but nobody works alone. The strategist frames the question. The designer and writer shape the answer together. The developer makes sure it holds up once real people use it.

## How we work

We work in three short cycles. First we frame: one or two weeks to agree on the decision the work must support. Then we shape: weekly drafts that you can open, read and react to. Finally we deliver: a steady rhythm of builds, handoffs and review points.

Every cycle ends with something concrete and a short list of open questions. You always know what changed, what is decided and what is still open.

## What we believe

Clear questions save more time than fast answers. Visible drafts beat polished surprises. A result is only finished when your team can maintain it without us.

## Say hello

Most engagements start with a one-hour call. Send us a short note about the decision you are facing, and we will reply within two working days with a few questions of our own.
