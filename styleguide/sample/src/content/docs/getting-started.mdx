---
title: About this catalog
---

Sample Styleguide is a standalone catalog of `@zudo-composer/ui`. Its components
and design tokens come from the installed, Git-pinned component pack.

The [component catalog](/components) starts each component with its Composer
sidecar defaults, followed by examples of its enum variants. Titles and
descriptions come from those sidecars. Categories follow the pack's folders:
Shared, Cards, Content, and Media.

The [design tokens](/tokens) show the pack's spacing, typography, and colors.
Previews use the pack's Composer stylesheet, including its markdown styling.
